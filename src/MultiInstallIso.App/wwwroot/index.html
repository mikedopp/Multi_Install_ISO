  1. Plan validates the definition and writes a plan with a SHA-256 hash. Nothing outside this PC is contacted.
  2. Answer media builds one small ISO per VM (autounattend.xml or ks.cfg), reads it back, and records hashes.
  3. Apply creates the VMs from the reviewed plan hash, one at a time, writing a receipt per VM.
